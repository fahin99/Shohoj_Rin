Project Management:
Visual Rules (Lecture 7, ER Notation Summary):
• Entity set: Rectangle (name only)
• Weak entity set: Double rectangle
• Attribute: Oval, linked to its entity
• Key attribute: Underlined oval
• Partial key (weak entity): Dashed-underlined oval
• Relationship: Diamond
• Relationship attribute: Oval linked to the diamond
• Identifying relationship: Double diamond
• Arrow on connector: "One" side of multiplicity
• Plain connector: "Many" side of multiplicity
No participation lines, no min/max labels — not covered in this lecture, so not drawn here.
